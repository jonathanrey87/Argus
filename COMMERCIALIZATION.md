# Astranyx Commercialization Strategy

Astranyx uses an open-core business model.

## Community edition

The command-line engine, public schemas, and core analyzers in this repository
are available under the Apache License 2.0. They provide a useful local-first
foundation, encourage integrations, and let prospective customers evaluate the
quality of the engine without sending private source code to a hosted service.

## Planned commercial products

Future paid products may include capabilities maintained outside the community
repository, such as:

- Branded PDF and client-delivery workflows
- Historical baselines and organization-wide project history
- Team collaboration, approvals, and role-based access
- Managed integrations, hosted orchestration, and support
- Compliance packs and consulting-oriented report templates

No feature is represented as available until it is implemented and documented.
Apache-licensed releases remain available under their published license; a
future commercial product does not revoke those rights.

## Brand and contributions

The Apache License grants copyright and patent permissions but does not grant
permission to use project trademarks as product branding. Before commercial
launch, register and publish a trademark policy for the Astranyx name and logo.

Contributors should certify that they have the right to submit their work. Add a
Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off process before accepting substantial
outside contributions, and obtain legal review before introducing contributor
agreements, dual licensing, or customer contract terms.

This document describes project strategy and is not legal advice or an offer of
commercial services.
